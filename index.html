Pairis Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026

This policy explains how Pairis handles your information. Pairis is developed and provided by Zhejiang Yinfa Energy Saving Technology Co., Ltd. ("we").

In short

  • We do not collect your personal information. Pairis has no accounts and no servers of its own.
  • Your recordings stay on your device, or on an external drive you choose. They are never uploaded to us.
  • Pairis has no ads or third-party analytics, and it does not track you.

Permissions Pairis uses

iOS manages these permissions, and you can turn any of them off in Settings at any time.

  • Camera: to shoot video. Without it you can't shoot, but the device can still be used as a remote.
  • Microphone: to record sound with your video. Without it you can still shoot, but recordings have no sound.
  • Motion data: for the level, picture orientation and dual-camera alignment. It is processed on the device. During a recording, some motion data may be written into the recording file so the picture can be realigned when you export it.
  • Local network: to find and connect to nearby devices, only when you use remote control.
  • Add to Photos: only when you choose to save a video from the system share sheet.
  • Photo picker: when you choose a sample picture on the color page, the system picker gives Pairis only the photo you selected, and a smaller copy is kept in the app. Pairis cannot read your other photos.

Information stored on your device

  • Recordings and related files: video, sound, covers, and recording information such as shooting parameters, color settings and alignment data.
  • Settings: shooting preferences, imported LUTs and tutorial progress.
  • Keychain: remote pairing keys, and the trial start time used to prevent repeat trials.

This information stays on your device and we cannot access it. iCloud Backup or a computer backup may include it; Apple handles that according to your backup settings.

Remote control

Remote control is off by default. It is active only when you turn on "Allow remote control" and pair, or use the device as a remote. Paired devices talk directly over the local network or a peer-to-peer connection. The connection is encrypted and does not pass through any server of ours or of a third party.

Paired devices may exchange:

  • device name and device identifier;
  • recording status and parameters, battery level and free storage;
  • remote commands;
  • a low frame rate preview picture, when you turn on preview on the remote;
  • the App Store purchase record and related device verification information, when Pro is shared.

Pair only with devices you trust, because they can see the preview and control recording on this device.

Purchases and trials

Purchases, trials and restores go through Apple's App Store. Apple handles your payment details and we do not receive them. Pairis only reads and checks the transaction information provided by the App Store, on the device, to decide whether Pro is available. See Apple's privacy policy for how Apple handles this information.

Sharing recordings

A recording leaves Pairis only when you use the system share sheet yourself. What happens to the file next depends on the app or service you choose.

Crash and diagnostic information

If you agree in iOS Settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may give us anonymous crash and performance statistics. You can change this in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

Retention and deletion

Because we do not hold your data, you control and delete it yourself:

  • Delete recordings in the Pairis gallery. Recordings on an external drive can also be deleted directly on the drive.
  • Uninstalling Pairis deletes the recordings and settings inside the app. Keychain information may remain on the device until the device is erased.
  • Revoke any of the permissions above in iOS Settings.

Third-party components

Pairis uses MetalFxKit, an open-source visual effects component that only draws interface effects and collects nothing. Pairis does not share your data with third parties.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. If a change affects how data is handled, we will tell you in the app.

Contact us

For questions or requests, email pairis.support@icloud.com.

Pairis 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 3 日

本政策说明 Pairis 如何处理你的信息。Pairis 由 Zhejiang Yinfa Energy Saving Technology Co., Ltd.（下称“我们”）开发和提供。

简要说明

  • 我们不收集你的个人信息。Pairis 没有账户系统，也没有自己的服务器。
  • 你的录像保存在你的设备上，或你选择的外置磁盘上，不会上传给我们。
  • Pairis 不含广告和第三方统计工具，也不会跟踪你。

Pairis 使用的权限

这些权限都由 iOS 管理，你可以随时在“设置”中关闭。

  • 相机：拍摄视频。不授权时无法拍摄，但这台设备仍可用作遥控器。
  • 麦克风：为视频录制声音。不授权时仍可拍摄，录像没有声音。
  • 运动数据：用于水平仪、画面方向和双摄对齐。数据只在设备上处理；开始录制后，部分运动数据可能写入录像文件，供导出成片时重新对齐画面。
  • 本地网络：只在你使用遥控功能时用于发现和连接附近的设备。
  • 添加到照片图库：只在你通过系统分享菜单选择存储视频时使用。
  • 选取照片：你在色彩页选择样张时，系统照片选取器只把你选中的那张交给 Pairis，缩小后的副本保存在 App 内。Pairis 无法读取你的其他照片。

存储在你设备上的信息

  • 录像和相关文件：包括视频、声音、封面，以及拍摄参数、色彩设置和对齐数据等录制信息。
  • 设置：包括拍摄偏好、导入的 LUT 和教程进度。
  • 钥匙串中的信息：遥控配对密钥，以及用于防止重复试用的试用开始时间。

这些信息留在你的设备上，我们无法访问。iOS 的 iCloud 云备份或电脑备份可能包含它们，这部分由 Apple 按你的备份设置处理。

遥控功能

遥控功能默认关闭。只有你打开“允许被遥控”并完成配对，或把设备用作遥控器时才会启用。配对的设备之间直接通过本地网络或点对点连接通信，连接经过加密，不经过我们或任何第三方的服务器。

配对的设备之间可能传输：

  • 设备名称和设备标识；
  • 拍摄状态和参数、电量和可用存储空间；
  • 遥控指令；
  • 你在遥控器上打开预览时的低帧率预览画面；
  • 共享 Pro 时的 App Store 购买凭证和相关的设备验证信息。

请只与你信任的设备配对，因为对方可以看到预览画面并控制这台设备录制。

购买与试用

购买、试用和恢复购买都通过 Apple 的 App Store 完成，付款信息由 Apple 处理，我们收不到。Pairis 只在设备上读取和校验 App Store 提供的交易信息，用来判断 Pro 是否可用。Apple 如何处理这些信息，请参阅 Apple 的隐私政策。

分享录像

只有你主动使用系统分享菜单时，录像才会离开 Pairis。文件之后如何处理，取决于你选择的 App 或服务。

崩溃和诊断信息

如果你在 iOS 设置中同意与 App 开发者共享分析数据，Apple 可能会向我们提供匿名的崩溃和性能统计。你可以在 iOS 的“设置 > 隐私与安全性 > 分析与改进”中更改这项选择。

保留和删除

因为我们不持有你的数据，你可以自己控制和删除：

  • 在 Pairis 相册里删除录像；外置磁盘上的录像也可以直接在磁盘上删除。
  • 卸载 Pairis 会删除 App 内的录像和设置。钥匙串中的信息可能保留在设备上，直到设备被抹掉。
  • 在 iOS“设置”中撤销以上任何权限。

第三方组件

Pairis 使用开源的视觉效果组件 MetalFxKit，它只用于绘制界面效果，不收集信息。Pairis 不会与第三方共享你的数据。

政策更新

如果本政策有变化，我们会更新此页面和生效日期。如果变化涉及数据处理方式，我们会在 App 内告知你。

联系我们

如有疑问或请求，请发送邮件至 pairis.support@icloud.com。
